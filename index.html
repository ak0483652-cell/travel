About us

A Kolkata travel desk for fixed-date group tours

Impression India is a travel agent and tour consultant at Bose Para Lane, Baghbazar. We run fixed departure tours, so the date, route and price are set before you book.

Every package states what it covers: stay, meals and main transport, with sleeper train tickets added on selected tours. You see the per-person cost up front.

Not sure which tour suits your leave dates or your group? Call us and we will help you choose.

How to read a package

6N / 7D
Nights and days. 6N / 7D means six nights and seven days.
Per Pax
Cost per person.
MAP
Meal plan with breakfast and dinner.
SL train
Sleeper class train ticket, included on selected tours.
3AC
Third AC train ticket, included on the Sanjay Tiger Reserve tour.
Kol to Kol
The tour starts and ends in Kolkata. Some start elsewhere, such as Port Blair or Srinagar.
